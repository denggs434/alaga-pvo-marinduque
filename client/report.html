Bitten or scratched? Wash the wound with soap and running water for 15 minutes.

Then go to the nearest Animal Bite Treatment Center today. For emergencies call the PVO hotline .

What happens next

  • Submitted — you get a reference number right away.
  • Under review — PVO staff check the report. Bites are handled first.
  • In progress — a team or the barangay is acting on it.
  • Resolved — we tell you the outcome in the report timeline.

Your privacy

Only you and PVO staff can see your report and contact details. The only public part is an optional missing-pet alert.
