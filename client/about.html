Why we exist

When we started, a pre-owned Tesla in the Philippines came with brochure range numbers and no way to check the most expensive part of the car. Buyers found out the truth after their first long drive.

So we bought the diagnostic tools, trained two technicians, and made one rule: no car goes on sale without a printed battery capacity test. We also bring in new cars that Tesla does not ship here officially, handle the import and LTO registration, and install home chargers anywhere on the island.

From port to driveway

How a Tesla reaches Mogpog

Every imported car takes the same trip. We handle each leg, so you only see the last one.

  1. Port of entryCleared through customs in Manila or Batangas, duties paid.
  2. LucenaTrucked south to Dalahican Port for the crossing.
  3. RoRo crossingAbout three hours on the ferry across Tayabas Bay.
  4. Balanacan PortRolls off in Mogpog and gets its first island charge.
  5. Voltyard showroomFive minutes up the road. Inspected, tested, listed.

Our story so far

Six years, one island.

From a borrowed garage to the island's first Cybertruck.

    A two-car garage in Mogpog

    Our first sale: a 2015 Model S brought over on the RoRo from Lucena.

  2. 2022
    Battery diagnostics in house

    Every pre-owned car now gets a printed capacity test before it is priced.

  3. 2023

    Room for twelve cars and two Wall Connectors for visitors.

    A Foundation Series, imported and LTO-registered by our team.

  5. 2025

Who we sell to

First-time EV owners

Teachers, nurses and business owners moving from a petrol car, who want a straight answer on charging at home since public chargers on the island are still rare.

OFW families

Families buying a car from abroad for parents back home. We send videos, the battery report and handle delivery.

Enthusiasts and collectors

Buyers after a Plaid, a Cybertruck, an original Roadster, or a build slot for the next one.

Resorts and businesses

Beach resorts, hotels and offices installing destination chargers for guests, especially during Moriones season.
