Caring for every animal on the island, together.

Alaga means "one you care for." It is the online home of the Provincial Veterinary Office (PVO) of Marinduque and the pet owners it serves.

For years, pet registrations lived in paper logbooks, vaccination drives were announced by word of mouth, and incidents were reported by phone or in person at the Capitol. Alaga brings all of that online: owners register pets and keep their health records, the PVO publishes schedules and advisories, and anyone with an account can report a bite, a missing pet or an animal in need.

What we work toward

Rabies-free Marinduque

Every dog and cat vaccinated every year, with records that follow the pet.

Responsible ownership

Registered pets, fewer strays, and help for owners who need it.

Fast response

Bites, missing pets and welfare cases reach the right team quickly, and reporters can see what we did.

A kind community

A wall where neighbours share their pets' lives and look out for each other's animals.

    Privacy

    We collect only what we need to register pets and handle reports, and we protect it under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

    • Public: pet photos, name, breed, age, municipality, anti-rabies status, owner's first name and last initial, and wall posts. Owners can make a pet private at any time.
    • Private (owner and PVO only): email, phone, barangay, vaccination records, medical history and every report.
    • Missing-pet alerts are public only if you choose; they never show your contact details.
    • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. You can ask the PVO to delete your account and pets.
