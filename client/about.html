Why we exist

Buying a used EV should not be a guess.

When we started, people buying a pre-owned Tesla in the Philippines had no way to check the most expensive part of the car. Sellers quoted range numbers from the brochure and buyers found out the truth after the first long drive.

So we bought the diagnostic tools, trained two technicians, and made one rule: no car goes on sale without a printed battery capacity test. Today we also bring in new cars that Tesla does not ship here officially, handle the import and LTO registration, and sell chargers and parts from the same showroom.

Who we sell to

First-time EV owners

Professionals in Metro Manila moving from a petrol car who want a clear explanation of charging at home and on the road.

Families

Parents looking at Model Y and Model X for school runs and provincial trips, who care about seats and range more than lap times.

Enthusiasts and collectors

Buyers after a Plaid, a Cybertruck, an original Roadster, or a build slot for the next one.

Businesses

Hotels, offices and condos installing destination chargers for guests and tenants.
