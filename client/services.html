What the PVO does for you

Veterinary services

The Provincial Veterinary Office protects animal and public health across Boac, Buenavista, Gasan, Mogpog, Santa Cruz and Torrijos. Most services for pet owners are free.

Pet registration

Register dogs, cats and other pets online and get a registration number at once. Registered pets are easier to return home when lost.

  • Free, online, any time
  • Health record kept on the profile
  • Verified by PVO staff

Anti-rabies vaccination

Free yearly anti-rabies shots for dogs and cats 3 months and older, at the office and at barangay drives, in line with the Anti-Rabies Act of 2007 (RA 9482).

  • Free at every drive
  • Recorded on your pet's profile
  • Booster every year

Frequently asked questions

Is pet registration really free?

Yes. Registration on Alaga, anti-rabies shots at PVO drives and deworming days are free. Some optional vaccines (such as 5-in-1) may be offered at a subsidised price.

Who can see my pet's health records?

Only you and PVO staff. Visitors see your pet's photos, name, breed, age, municipality and whether the anti-rabies vaccine is up to date. They see your first name and last initial, never your contact details.

I added a vaccination myself. Why does it say "Owner entry"?

Vaccinations you add are kept as your own record. When you visit the PVO with the vaccination card, staff can mark them as verified.

My pet is lost. What should I do?

File a Missing pet report. You can choose to show a missing-pet alert on the community wall. People who spot your pet call the PVO and quote your reference number, so your number stays private.

Can I register a pet for someone without internet access?

Yes, they can visit the office, or you can help them create an account. Each account can register up to 20 pets.
